# Pyxis Support

Pyxis is a local-first closet organization app for iPhone. It stores clothing photos, cutouts, metadata, saved fits, and on-device memory records in the app's local container on your device.

## Contact

## Helpful Details

When reporting an issue, include:

- The iPhone model.
- The iOS version.
- The Pyxis app version and build number.
- The action you were taking when the issue happened.
- Whether the issue happens again after quitting and reopening Pyxis.

Do not send private clothing photos unless you are comfortable sharing them for support.

## Local Data

Pyxis does not create accounts, run analytics, or sync the closet database to a cloud service. Closet photos remain local unless you explicitly tap an AI generation action; that action sends the disclosed person and/or garment photos through the Pyxis gateway to xAI. xAI may retain API requests and responses for up to 30 days under its standard data controls. Deleting the app removes data stored in the app's local container from the device but does not shorten that temporary provider retention window.
