## Details

- Compose saved fits from their local closet images in a two-column lookbook in both light and dark appearances.
- Search by fit name, notes, and clothing metadata; filter favorites, garment color, and season; sort by recency or wear count.
- Hide soft-deleted fits and preserve the existing detail and Build actions. No outfit storage migration or remote image upload is involved.
- Add a light and dark Build stage with a swipeable garment ring and an optional, session-only personal photo. The existing try-on action remains the explicit way to generate a dressed preview.
