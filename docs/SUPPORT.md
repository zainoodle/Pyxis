# Pyxis Support

Pyxis is a local-first closet organization app for iPhone. It stores clothing photos, cutouts, metadata, saved fits, and on-device memory records in the app's local container on your device.

## Helpful Details

When reporting an issue, include:

- The iPhone model.
- The iOS version.
- The Pyxis app version and build number.
- The action you were taking when the issue happened.
- Whether the issue happens again after quitting and reopening Pyxis.

Do not send private clothing photos unless you are comfortable sharing them for support.

## Local Data

Pyxis does not create accounts, run analytics, or sync the closet database. Outfit on you uploads only your chosen reference and clothing images after consent, using xAI zero-retention processing. Remembered photos and saved previews stay on device; remove them in the try-on screen or Saved Previews. AI garment cleanup is separate and may retain requests/responses at xAI for up to 30 days.

## Outfit on you

Choose a full-body photo, add clothes from your closet or Photos, check the suggested garment types, and tap Try On. A paid monthly subscription includes a limited allowance shown in the app. Failed generations do not consume the allowance; a completed generation can still count if your connection drops before the result arrives. The app refreshes your balance after an error. Save a result before trying another version. The preview does not guarantee sizing or fit.

Use Restore Purchases if an active subscription is missing, and Manage Subscription to review Apple's billing controls. Subscription verification and allowance records are retained until 35 days after the recorded billing period ends unless continued use updates the period. Deleting Pyxis does not cancel a subscription. If try-on says it is coming soon, generation is unavailable; your local closet and saved previews still work.
